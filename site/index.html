A few helpful answers

Before we
get started.

Is the website really free?

Yes. There’s no design or build fee. What’s the catch? There is none. We earn our keep hosting and caring for your site, so you just pick a monthly plan: Starter is $99/month, Plus is $129/month, and Premium is $149/month. Domain registration and renewal are paid separately by you.

How fast will my website be live?

Within 24 hours of receiving all your info. The clock starts once we have everything we need, so the sooner you send it, the sooner you’re live.

What do you need from me?

Your business details, the services you offer, your logo if you have one, and any photos you’d like to use. We’ll walk you through exactly what to send on our first call.

When does monthly hosting start?

Hosting starts when your website goes live. Nothing is due upfront.

What’s the difference between Starter, Plus, and Premium?

Every plan includes a professional website of up to five pages, hosting, SSL, backups, security updates, uptime monitoring, and email support. Starter ($99/month) includes 1 content update request per month. Plus ($129/month) includes 3 update requests per month completed within 3 business days, built-in local SEO, a monthly site health report, and a quarterly speed tune-up. Premium ($149/month) includes 5 update requests per month completed within 2 business days, up to eight pages, priority phone and text support, a quarterly growth report, and stock photo sourcing.

What’s included?

A custom-designed website built around your business (up to five pages, eight on Premium), a mobile-friendly layout, a contact form, click-to-call links, and hosting with security and upkeep, all covered by your monthly plan.

What if I want something fully custom?

We still build fully custom websites for bigger projects. Those are quoted as a flat price up front, with a 50% deposit to start and the remaining 50% due when your website goes live.
