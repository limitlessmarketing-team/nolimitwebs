A few helpful answers

Before we
get started.

What happens after I request a mockup?

We’ll get in touch within one business day to learn about your business and what you need. Then we’ll create a free custom concept so you can see the direction before committing.

How do payments work?

We agree on your total project price up front. You pay a 50% deposit to start the build, with the remaining 50% due when your website goes live.

When does monthly hosting start?

Your first hosting payment starts 30 days after launch and continues monthly. We agree on the hosting price with you before you commit.

How long will my website take?

Timing depends on the scope of your website and how quickly content and feedback are ready. We’ll agree on a timeline with you before the build begins.

Am I committed after seeing the mockup?

No. The custom mockup is free, with no obligation. If you like the direction, we’ll agree on the scope and pricing before moving forward.
