Privacy Policy

Limitless Marketing Group LLC operates nolimitwebs.com and provides website design and hosting services. This policy explains how we handle information collected through this website and our related communications.

Information we collect

When you contact us or request a mockup, we collect the information you choose to provide, such as your name, business name, email address, telephone number, and project details. If you choose text updates, we keep your consent choice, the number provided, the date, the form source, and the disclosure shown to you. Our hosting and security providers process technical information such as IP addresses, browser information, and request logs to operate the site and prevent abuse.

How we use information

We use your information to respond to inquiries, prepare requested mockups and quotes, schedule appointments, provide project and hosting services, administer billing, keep consent and opt-out records, and protect our services. Providing a phone number does not by itself enroll you in text messages.

Text messaging privacy

We do not sell, rent, or share mobile information, SMS opt-in data, or text messaging consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. SMS consent is not transferred to another business. We may provide the information needed to deliver our messages to service providers acting on our behalf, solely to operate our messaging service.

Service providers and other disclosures

We use providers for website hosting and security, database storage, email delivery, customer relationship management, communications, and payment processing. These include Cloudflare, Supabase, Resend, Close and its messaging providers, and Stripe. We provide information as needed for these services. Stripe handles payment details through its checkout; do not send payment-card details through our contact form. We may also disclose information where required by law or to protect rights and security. These disclosures do not permit sharing SMS consent for another party's marketing.

Your choices

Retention and security
